📶 The-Finance-Leader

The Finance Leader is a web-based household financial planning application.

The main idea is:
    A family enters its financial information and future goals, and the system analyses the situation to suggest how the family can plan its money more effectively.

For example, a household may have:
*Monthly income/salary*
Existing EMIs
Insurance premiums
Current savings
Essential household expenses
Future financial goals
🏠 Buying a house
🪙 Purchasing gold
💍 Wedding expenses
🎓 Education
Other major goals
